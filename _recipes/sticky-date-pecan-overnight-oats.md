---
title: Sticky Date & Pecan Overnight Oats
category: Breakfast
emoji: " 🍯"
description: Sweet, salty and sticky overnight oats!
time: 5 mins + overnight
ingredients: |
  ¼ cup rolled oats
  ½ cup milk of choice
  1 tbsp chia seeds
  1 tbsp maple syrup
  2 tbsp pecans, roughly chopped
  1 tsp vanilla essence
  ½ tsp cinnamon
  3 dates, pitted
  1 tsp tahini
  Pinch of Maldon salt
method: >-
  Combine all ingredients, ensuring the maple syrup is evenly mixed through.
  Refrigerate overnight. In the morning, top with extra pecans and  drizzle with
  tahini and more maple, if desired.


  Maldon salt is the star ingredient - it brings out and balances the sweetness!
---
