---
title: Almond Croissant Overnight Oats
category: Breakfast
emoji: " 🥐"
description: The cosy warmth of an almond croissant, without the pastry.
time: 5 mins + overnight
ingredients: |
  ¼ cup rolled oats
  ½ cup almond milk
  2 tbsp ground almonds
  1 tbsp chia seeds
  2tbsp sultanas
  1 tbsp almond butter
  1 tsp almond essence
  ½ tsp cinnamon
method: Mix all ingredients except the flaked almonds. Stir until the almond
  butter is well combined. Refrigerate overnight. Top with flaked almonds in the
  morning, toast them in a dry pan for extra flavour.
---
