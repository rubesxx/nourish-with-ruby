---
title: Matcha, Raspberry & Cacao Overnight Oats
category: Breakfast
emoji: " 🍵"
description: Earthy matcha, tart raspberries and bitter cacao nibs!
time: 5. ins + overnight
ingredients: |
  ¼ cup rolled oats
  ½ cup milk of choice
  3 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  1 tbsp chia seeds
  1 tsp matcha powder
  Zest of ½ lemon
  Handful of fresh or frozen raspberries
method: Whisk the matcha into the milk until dissolved. Combine with oats,
  yoghurt, chia seeds and lemon zest. Fold in the raspberries gently.
  Refrigerate overnight. Top with cacao nibs before eating.
---
