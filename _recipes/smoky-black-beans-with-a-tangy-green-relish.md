---
title: Smoky Black Beans with a Tangy Green Relish
category: Dinner
emoji: " 🫘"
description: Hearty black beans and rice - a complete source of protein and
  topped with all the textures - crispy kale, tangy pickled onion, indulgent
  sour cream and feta, a flavour exploding green relish and toasted seeds.
time: 30 mins
ingredients: |
  For The Beans: 
  2 tins black beans, drained
  1 tin coconut milk 
  1 leek
  2 mixed peppers, sliced
  2 onions, diced
  4 garlic cloves
  1 tsp smoked paprika
  A handful of coriander stems
  Thumb sized piece of ginger
  Lime juice, salt

  Rice:
  Basmati rice
  Desiccated coconut
  Coconut oil

  For The Toppings:
  Crispy kale (tossed in oil, baked 10 mins)
  Toasted seeds
  Pickled red onion
  Sour cream or yoghurt
  Feta, crumbled

  For The Cashew Herb Relish:
  A handful each of coriander and parsley
  ¼ cup raw cashews, soaked 1 hour
  1 tbsp jalapeño brine
  Juice of 1 lime
  Glug of olive oil
method: >-
  Method


  Start pickled onions: Finely chop the red onions and place in a jar. Add the salt, sugar, juice of half a lime, and vinegar. Seal and shake to combine. Set aside. The longer they sit, the better.


  Fry the onions: Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onions and cook, stirring occasionally, until golden. Season well with salt and pepper.


  Add aromatics: Add the garlic, ginger, and coriander stems to the pan and fry for a further 5 minutes, stirring regularly.


  Add leek and peppers: Add the leek and cook until softened. Then add the peppers. Using a mix of colours boosts the polyphenol profile. Fry until the peppers begin to soften.


  Add black beans: Add the black beans and a good pinch of salt. Fry everything together for about 5 minutes.


  Simmer with coconut milk: Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a boil. Let it reduce and thicken for 15 to 20 minutes. Finish with lime juice to taste.


  Roast the kale: Preheat your oven to 200C / 180C fan. Toss the kale with oil and a pinch of salt on a baking tray. Roast until crispy.


  Make cashew relish: Blend the cashews, garlic, lime juice, and water together until smooth. Adjust consistency with extra water as needed. Season with salt.


  Toast coconut for rice: In a saucepan, toast the desiccated coconut in coconut oil over medium heat for about 2 minutes until fragrant and golden.


  Toast and cook rice: Add the well-rinsed rice to the pan and toast with the coconut for a further 2 minutes. Then add water as usual and cook until done.


  Assemble and serve: Spoon coconut rice into bowls. Top with the black bean curry, crispy kale, pickled onions, sour cream and feta and a generous dollop of cashew relish. Add extra lime if desired.


  Notes

  The pickled onions benefit from at least 30 minutes in the jar, so make them first, or even the day before. For the cashew relish, soaking the cashews in water for an hour first gives a creamier result. Use red, yellow, and orange peppers together for maximum polyphenol variety.
