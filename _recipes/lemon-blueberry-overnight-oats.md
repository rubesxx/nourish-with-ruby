---
title: Lemon Blueberry Overnight Oats
category: Breakfast
emoji: 🫐
description: Smashed juicy blueberrys and tangy lemon zest, stirred into a
  creamy overnight oats base. Unusual, and tasty like a dessert - this breakfast
  is a trip for your tastebuds!
time: 5 mins
ingredients: |
  ¼ cup rolled oats
  ½ cup almond milk
  3 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  1 tbsp chia seeds
  1 tbsp desiccated coconut
  Zest of ½ lemon
  1 tsp vanilla essence
  A handful of blueberries
method: >-
  Smash the blueberries in the bottom of a jar or bowl. Add all remaining
  ingredients and stir well. Seal and refrigerate overnight. In the morning,
  give it a stir and add a splash more milk if needed. Top with fresh
  blueberries and extra coconut.


  Tip: The smashed blueberries act as a natural sweetener and soak into the oats overnight. No added sugar needed. Pair with Greek yoghurt for a protein hit that keeps you full until lunch.
